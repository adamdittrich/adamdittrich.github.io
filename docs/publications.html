Measuring Financial Stress under the Gold Standard: Evidence from Interwar Czechoslovakia

Adam Dittrich

A monthly composite index uses eleven financial and macro-financial indicators to recover the chronology of pressure from November 1929 to September 1938. The project connects statistical measurement to the institutional mechanics of the Czechoslovak gold-standard regime.
