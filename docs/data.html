Data & replication

Evidence designed to be reused

Historical data construction is part of the substantive contribution. Each public project documents coverage, source logic, transformations, and limitations so that the evidence can be inspected beyond a single regression table.

Dataset 01Monthly · Czechoslovakia · 1929–1938

Measuring Financial Stress under the Gold Standard

A monthly dataset and composite index designed to recover the chronology of monetary, banking, and macro-financial pressure in interwar Czechoslovakia.

11 component indicators

107 monthly observations

1929:11 series begins

1938:09 series ends

Research use

  • identifying distinct phases of financial pressure;
  • comparing market, monetary, and real-economy chronologies;
  • evaluating conventional dates assigned to the Czechoslovak Depression;
  • studying monetary defence before and after devaluation.

Package contents

  • underlying monthly indicators;
  • transformed and standardised series;
  • composite financial-stress measure;
  • documentation and replication materials;
  • transparent versioned DOI.

Dataset 02Annual · Europe · 1927–1938

European bilateral trade, 1927–1938

Dataset: Commercial Hysteresis: Persistence and Reorganization in Europe’s Bilateral Trade Geography during the Great Depression.

A bilateral panel for tracing persistence, destruction, and reorganisation in Europe’s commercial relationships across the Great Depression.

21 European economies

12 annual cross-sections

1927 pre-crisis benchmark

1938 late-interwar endpoint

Research use

  • separating aggregate trade collapse from link-level persistence;
  • mapping partner substitution and regional reorientation;
  • estimating intensive- and extensive-margin adjustment;
  • studying the effects of exchange and clearing regimes.

Unit of observation

The core unit is the directional country-pair-year. That structure permits exporter, importer, pair, and time effects while preserving the geography of commercial adjustment.

Open dataset · DOI 10.3886/E247132V2

Dataset 03Monthly · Europe · 1925–1936

The Missing J

The Missing J in Interwar Europe: A Monthly Cliometric Dataset on Effective Exchange Rates and Trade-Balance Adjustment, 1925–1936.

Adam Dittrich · openICPSR · 2026

A monthly European dataset linking exports, imports, prices, economic activity, and exchange rates to the study of trade-balance adjustment. Annual bilateral trade weights drawn from League of Nations trade reports support the construction of effective exchange rates.

Coverage

Monthly observations for 1925–1936, with linked annual bilateral trade information. Variable availability and country coverage should be checked in the deposit documentation before building a particular estimation sample.

Research use

Comparing nominal and real effective exchange rates, tracing the timing of trade-balance responses, and distinguishing exchange-rate adjustment from changes in prices and economic activity.

Open dataset · DOI 10.3886/E251239V1

The public packages sit within a broader effort to consolidate scattered evidence on the interwar Czechoslovak economy. These components are under active construction and will be released only when their provenance and transformations are sufficiently documented.

Reconstructing

Harmonising

Population, occupational structure, social and political indicators, and electoral outcomes for the 1920s and 1930s.

Replication principles

A dataset should expose its judgement calls

Provenance

Every series should be traceable to an archival table, statistical publication, or documented secondary reconstruction.

Transformations

Deflation, interpolation, seasonal adjustment, harmonisation, and index construction should be visible rather than buried.

Uncertainty

Alternative assumptions and fragile links belong in the package, not only in private working files.

Versioning

Stable repositories and versioned DOIs make it possible to distinguish a cited release from later improvements.
