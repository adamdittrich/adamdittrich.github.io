Research and data

National accounts
Czechoslovak contribution to a comparative Central and Eastern European GDP-reconstruction project with Matthias Morys and Marcin Wroński, focusing on agricultural output and value added, prices, intermediate inputs, and cross-country comparability.
Monetary history
Reconstruction of monthly Czechoslovak monetary and macroeconomic series from National Bank reports, 1926–1938, alongside comparative European sources.
Open research
Public datasets on Czechoslovak financial stress and monthly European exchange-rate and trade-balance adjustment (The Missing J, 1925–1936). Data and replication packages.
Manuscripts
Current research on monetary defence, trade adjustment, labour markets, historical national accounts, and Czech economic thought. Publications and current papers.

Methods and training

Historical source criticism; archival, parliamentary and press research; central-bank balance-sheet reconstruction; index construction and decomposition; time-series and panel econometrics; PPML and gravity models; event studies; synthetic control and comparative counterfactual analysis.
