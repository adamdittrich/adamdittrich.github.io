07

Sources, definitions and coverage

The historical labels matter. These notes identify the populations and explain the principal boundaries of the reported quantities.

A geographical scope

The annual aggregate covers joint-stock commercial banks in Bohemia, Moravia and Silesia, including Czech, German and mixed-ownership institutions. It excludes the separately reported eastern institutions, ordinary savings banks and cooperative credit associations; joint-stock central institutions can belong. Branches and claims need not all be located within those lands.

Two observation units

The League aggregate changes with entry, mergers, liquidation and reporting. The handbook panel follows fourteen named institutions represented in the 1938 handbook and therefore does not identify exits. It is not a bank-by-bank disaggregation of the League series.

Capital and credit

Published capital combines paid capital with reported reserves. Separately recorded funds, hidden reserves and valuation conventions prevent it from measuring all economic loss-bearing resources. Lending-related categories include claims on financial institutions as well as other borrowers.

Income and missing values

Income is an annual flow; assets are end-year stocks. The League's 1936 income and balance-sheet reporting counts differ, so the corresponding aggregate income-to-assets ratios are left blank. Zero, source dashes and unavailable observations are distinguished in the source records.

Bank-specific accounting notes

The savings-bank central institution includes Treasury bills within its cash category. Legiobanka's deposit line includes mortgage borrowing. Všeobecná družstevní banka's securities include participations; its original thousand-crown values have been converted to millions. Moravská banka's 1933 asset components fall Kč 20 million short of its reported total, which is supported by the liability sum. The published total is retained and flagged.

Reserve histories record transfers at Escomp in 1935 and Länderbanka in 1937, and opening-balance adjustments at other institutions. Their amounts are not automatically corrections to the aggregate capital total. See the reserve-events table and variable dictionary.

The source base

  1. League of Nations. Commercial-bank balance sheets, income accounts and accompanying notes in the 1931, 1934, 1935, 1938 and 1939 banking volumes. The reconstruction records the selected publication IDs, table entries and page locations for the annual observations.
  2. Josef Kašpar, 1938. Československé bursovní papíry 1938–1939, annual bank tables for 1933–1937. The consulted digital edition is a modern retyping, with metadata dated 2006. The observation records identify its page locations.
  3. Státní úřad statistický, 1932. Statistická příručka republiky Československé IV, especially pp. 259–260, supports the 1929 institution crosswalk and reserve-category comparisons.
  4. Monetary context. League monetary reviews, BIS annual reports and National Bank material supply contextual observations and institutional descriptions. These tables do not change the two core reporting populations.

The downloads include a variable dictionary, source index, population definitions, status flags and validation records. Historical publications remain cited at their original locations; this page contains reconstructed observations and derived displays.
