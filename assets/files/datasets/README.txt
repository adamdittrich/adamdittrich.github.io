Source: the accompanying author-supplied Excel workbook. Numeric values are the stored cell values. Formulas and visual formatting are not included. Dates are ISO YYYY-MM-DD; missing values are blank. No data are interpolated, revised or reconciled.

Trade: values are million current Kč; percentage columns store percentage numbers, not fractions. Annual indices use the annual 1929 value; monthly indices use the mean month in 1929. National annual totals need not equal sums of the monthly series. See foreign-trade-frequency-check.csv. Partner shares refer to national totals; the 15 partners are not an exhaustive world panel.

EER: 1929=100; higher means depreciation. Preferred indices use current-year geometric chains. NEER 1920–1938; REER 1923–1938; analytical core 1925–1938. Weights and coverage are proportions from 0 to 1. The EER basket has 14 partners and is not the same as the foreign-trade partner sample. The early extension is preserved unchanged.

DOI cited by the EER workbook: https://doi.org/10.3886/E251023V1. That DOI identifies the related deposit, not a newly registered identifier for these reader files.
